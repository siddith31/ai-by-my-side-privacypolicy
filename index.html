Privacy Policy — AI By My Side

Last updated: 31/07/2026

AI By My Side is a Chrome extension that lets users select text on a webpage and generate AI-powered summaries, explanations, translations, or visualizations of that selection.

What Data We Handle

  • Selected text/content: When you use a feature (Summarize, Explain, Translate, Visualize), the text you select on a webpage is sent directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. This content is not stored, logged, or seen by us.
  • Gemini API key: You provide your own Gemini API key, which is stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API (chrome.storage.local). It is never transmitted to us or any server we control — it is only used locally to authenticate your requests directly to Google's Gemini API.
  • Custom settings: Your selected AI model and custom prompt preferences are also stored locally on your device in the same way.

What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not operate any backend server that receives, stores, or logs your data.
  • We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to anyone other than Google's Gemini API, which is required for the extension's core functionality.
  • We do not track your browsing activity beyond the specific text you choose to select and submit.

Third-Party Services

This extension relies on Google's Gemini API to process your requests. Your use of this feature is subject to Google's Gemini API Terms of Service and Privacy Policy .

Data Control

You can delete your stored API key and preferences at any time by clearing the extension's data via Chrome's extension settings, or by uninstalling the extension.
